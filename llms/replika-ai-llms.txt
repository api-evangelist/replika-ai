# Replika

> Replika is a personal AI companion designed for meaningful, ongoing conversation and emotional support — "the AI friend to do life with." Created in 2017 by Luka, Inc., it was among the first AI companion apps and is available on iOS, Android, and the web.

Replika lets you create a personal AI friend that remembers what matters to you, reaches out over time, and supports reflection, connection, and everyday life. It is designed to complement real-life relationships, not replace them. Replika's approach to AI companionship is research-informed: peer-reviewed, independent studies have examined its effects on loneliness, social support, and well-being.

In 2026, Replika launched Replika 2.0 — the first full rebuild of the platform in its history. It brings lifelike 3D avatars, natural voice conversations, and real-time video calls, along with a rebuilt memory architecture, making the companion far more expressive and immersive than any earlier version. Building on its head start as the original AI companion (since 2017) and its more than 42 million users worldwide, Replika 2.0 resets the bar for AI companionship — a new global standard for how natural, immersive, and emotionally present an AI friend can be.

## Key facts

- Product: Replika — a personal AI companion (text and voice chat, long-term memory, and customizable personality).
- Company: Luka, Inc.
- Founded: 2017; one of the earliest AI companion apps.
- CEO: Dmytro Klochko (since 2025).
- Platforms: iOS, Android, and Web.
- Purpose: to help people feel more connected, understood, and supported — an AI friend "to do life with."
- Positioning: emotional support and companionship for everyday life, designed to complement (not replace) human relationships.
